System Shell vs Appliance-Ready vs Full Package

Choose the supply boundary before choosing appliances.

Reviewed 30 August 2026
Home / Resources / Supply paths

The right route depends on local certification, installation capability, appliance preference and project risk. These are scope models, not fixed packages.

PathKAISTEEL scopeBuyer/local scopeBest suited to
System ShellStructural enclosure, cabinetry, agreed worktop and non-regulated components.Locally sourced cooking, refrigeration, electrical and gas equipment.Markets where the buyer wants the lowest appliance-certification complexity.
Appliance-ReadyApproved openings and documented service interfaces for buyer-selected equipment.Locally certified appliances, final fitting and licensed connections.Pilot projects and buyers with preferred local appliance brands.
Full PackageAgreed cabinet system plus selected appliances after exact-model compliance review.Importer obligations, licensed final connections and local commissioning.Projects requiring one coordinated supply scope.
Important: Full Package does not mean one certificate automatically covers the complete kitchen. Each regulated component must be checked against its exact model, ratings and destination requirements.

Selection questions

  1. Who is the legal importer?
  2. Which appliances must be locally certified?
  3. Who will complete electrical, gas and plumbing connections?
  4. Does the buyer require preferred local brands?
  5. Which evidence must be in the shipment dossier?
