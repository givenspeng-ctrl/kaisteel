Modular vs fully assembled outdoor kitchen shipping

The trade-off is container utilization versus destination assembly—not a universal freight percentage.

Reviewed 30 August 2026
Home / Resources / Shipping
FactorFully assembledModular
Container utilizationLower unit count because assembled volume includes unused internal space.Potentially higher unit count when components can be protected and nested safely.
Destination workLower assembly requirement.Requires approved instructions, tools, trained labor and final checks.
Packing designComplete-unit protection and restraint.Component identification, edge protection, hardware control and assembly sequence.
Decision basisLanded cost, damage risk, labor cost, installation capability and project schedule.

How KAISTEEL confirms loading

  1. Freeze the final product configuration.
  2. Approve packaging construction and external dimensions.
  3. Record gross weight and handling constraints.
  4. Create a model-specific loading plan.
  5. Confirm final quantity and freight assumptions in the quotation.
PRO and MAX catalogue figures are preliminary targets. CUSTOM and changed configurations require a new calculation.
