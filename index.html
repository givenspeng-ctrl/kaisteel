About & transparency

A clear contracting party for international B2B projects.

KAISTEEL LIMITED is a Hong Kong-registered brand and B2B seller of integrated outdoor kitchen systems — a clear contracting party for quotations, contracts, technical coordination and after-sales communication.

Production is supported by established manufacturing capabilities in Foshan, Guangdong. Every order is controlled through approved specifications, drawings and an order-specific Compliance Dossier.
