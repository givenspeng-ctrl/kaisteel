Buyer questions

Before requesting a quotation.

Two points buyers commonly confirm before moving to configuration review.

Can KAISTEEL provide certification and compliance documents?

KAISTEEL offers a System Shell supply path designed to reduce certification complexity at import: we supply the structural enclosure, cabinetry, worktop and agreed non-appliance components, with interfaces prepared for buyer-selected, locally certified appliances sourced in the destination market.

Final electrical, gas and plumbing connections remain the responsibility of licensed local tradespeople. KAISTEEL does not itself hold CE, UL, SAA or CSA marks for the complete system — see the full certification disclaimer and System Shell vs. Full Package comparison.

Have you supplied customers in the United States?

Our manufacturing side has previously produced outdoor-kitchen products for US customers on an OEM/private-label basis. KAISTEEL has not yet shipped to the United States under its own brand. We also support OEM and private-label programs.

Customer identities are not disclosed without prior written consent. Relevant manufacturing, specification and quality-control information can be provided during qualified project due diligence.

How are appliance openings and interfaces determined?

For Appliance-Ready supply, cut-outs, clearances, utility interfaces and fixing points are not standardized across all projects. They are determined from the buyer's exact appliance brand and model, the manufacturer's installation documentation, applicable local regulations and the approved order drawings.

How many PRO or MAX units fit in a 40'HQ container?

Fully assembled outdoor kitchens occupy significant volume with trapped air. On current packing engineering, a 40'HQ can carry approximately 13 fully assembled PRO units or up to 26 modular PRO units (MAX: 8 assembled / 16 modular).

These figures are preliminary and confirmed on the final approved packing plan for each configuration. See the dedicated PRO vs MAX buyer page or our modular vs. assembled shipping guide for loading and freight detail.
