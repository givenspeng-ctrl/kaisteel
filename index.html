Shipping risk & transit damage remedy

Risk transfer: Risk passes per the agreed Incoterm — FOB from a Pearl River Delta port (Nansha, Shekou or Yantian, as agreed) unless otherwise specified. CIF with marine cargo insurance can be arranged on request.

Transit damage remedy: Report visible or concealed transit damage with photographs and supporting evidence within 7 days of container devanning. Once the claim is reviewed and accepted, replacement stainless panels, doors and worktops are supplied free of product charge, consolidated into your next shipment or dispatched separately on request. Freight, insurance, customs duties, import taxes, clearance charges and destination costs remain the buyer's responsibility unless otherwise agreed in writing; removal, reinstallation and on-site labour are not included. This remedy does not limit any claim available under the agreed Incoterm, carrier liability or cargo insurance.

Production capability

Manufacturing behind every unit.

Production is coordinated through an approved manufacturing partner in Foshan, Guangdong, under KAISTEEL's order-specific specification, QC and documentation controls. Quotations, technical coordination and after-sales are handled directly by KAISTEEL LIMITED. Supplier history and facility qualifications are verified through controlled due diligence before being used for an order.

15Years of founder-led experience in the outdoor product sector
304 / 316Project-selected grades with matched evidence
FoshanManufacturing base
TestedProject-specific test and certification support

304 or 316 stainless steel is available to order across the full PRO, MAX and CUSTOM range — not limited to CUSTOM projects. 304 is the standard grade; 316 can be specified as an order-selected upgrade for coastal or higher-corrosion environments. Coating requirements and destination-specific appliance packages can also be specified.

Appliance certification sits with the component supplier. Cabinet and system integration is controlled at the manufacturing site. KAISTEEL issues the order-specific Compliance Dossier matched to the approved BOM and destination market — see Trade Documents for the controlled set, or the full Compliance & Manufacturing Dossier for the complete material table and evidence policy.

Facility-level detail beyond the published dossier — full audit access, additional process documentation — can be shared under a mutual NDA for qualified buyers evaluating a production-scale order. Third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, Bureau Veritas or a buyer-appointed inspector) is welcome on any order, at the buyer's cost, and can be arranged directly with the production site — shipment release can be agreed to follow a passed inspection.
