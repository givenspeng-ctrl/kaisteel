Compliance policy

Who is responsible for what.

Compliance responsibility: Equipment certification is provided by the appliance or gas-equipment supplier; cabinet and system integration compliance is controlled by the manufacturing site; KAISTEEL compiles and transfers the order-specific compliance dossier; the appointed importer or distributor fulfils destination-market legal obligations; and final electrical, gas and plumbing connections are completed by licensed local tradespeople. Certificate holder, exact model scope, ratings, validity and market acceptance are verified for each order.

Order-specific dossier

What ships with every confirmed order.

For each confirmed production lot or shipment, KAISTEEL compiles an order-specific Compliance Dossier matched to the approved BOM, selected components and destination market.

Material batch recordsApplicable SUS304/SUS316 evidence and structural-material declarations linked to the approved order.
Coating batch recordsCoating system, color/batch references and available film-thickness inspection records.
Applicable type testsSalt-spray and UV/weathering reports matched to the relevant substrate and coating system where available.
Exact-model certificatesElectrical and gas equipment documents checked against brand, model, rating, fuel type and destination scope.
Unit-level release evidenceNameplates, serial numbers and final QC records for the actual supplied configuration.
Packing & shipment evidenceCase identification, packing records and loading evidence prepared for the confirmed shipment.
Document timing: Required materials, tests and certifications are defined at Technical Freeze and completed during the production cycle. Material and coating records are batch-linked. Salt-spray and UV reports may be applicable type-test documents or project-specific tests, as agreed. Appliance and gas certificates are exact-model documents checked for scope and validity on each order. The indexed Compliance Dossier must be complete before shipment release.
