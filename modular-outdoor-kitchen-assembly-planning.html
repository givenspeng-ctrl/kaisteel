KAISTEEL recorded PRO and MAX trial assemblies using two people and approximately four hours per unit under trial conditions. Actual site results vary with configuration, access, unloading, lifting equipment, installer familiarity, weather and utility work.

Planning areaWhat must be confirmed
Structure and materialsQ235 galvanized primary frame, powder-coated aluminum exterior, applicable aluminum honeycomb construction and order-selected 304 or 316 stainless-steel components against the approved BOM.
InterfacesApplicable supplied electrical interfaces use keyed connectors designed to reduce incorrect mating; voltage, connector and component scope remain order-specific.
HandlingPackage access, unloading method, crew, tools, lifting aids, safe work area and component weights.
Final utilitiesGas, electrical, water and drainage connections completed or verified by appropriately licensed local trades where required.

Typical control sequence

  1. Inspect packages and record visible transit damage.
  2. Identify components against the packing list and approved drawings.
  3. Position, connect, level and secure structural sections.
  4. Install upper assemblies using the confirmed lifting plan.
  5. Install cabinet, finish-panel, worktop and approved appliance components.
  6. Complete mechanical and supplied-component function checks.
  7. Arrange regulated utility connections and local commissioning.
The issued assembly guide, approved BOM, drawings and order-specific document index govern the supplied configuration.
