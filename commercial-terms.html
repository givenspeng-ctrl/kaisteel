Commercial policy

Commercial Terms, Payments & Cancellations

This policy update takes effect on 11 November 2026, 00:00 HKT (UTC+8). Existing orders remain governed by their agreed documents and applicable statutory rights.

These terms apply to business-to-business enquiries and orders. The final written quotation, proforma invoice (PI), sales contract and approved technical documents govern the specific order and prevail where they differ from this summary.

1. Made-to-order B2B supply

KAISTEEL outdoor kitchen systems are supplied to professional buyers on a made-to-order basis. A product configuration, price, scope of supply, packing method, lead time, Incoterms, payment terms and technical requirements are confirmed only in the applicable quotation and order documents.

2. Order formation

An RFQ, email exchange, brochure, indicative configuration or payment discussion is not a binding offer. An order is accepted only when KAISTEEL LIMITED issues or countersigns the applicable PI, sales contract or other written order confirmation, and the agreed technical scope is confirmed.

3. Payment

Order-specific payment terms

Payment amounts, proportions and milestones are determined per order in the KAISTEEL-approved quotation, PI or contract. The agreed deposit must actually be received before production scheduling. Eligible sample credit is deducted from the pre-shipment balance, and the remaining balance must be paid in full before shipment. Payments use approved KAISTEEL LIMITED business channels.

  • Payment instructions and secure payment links are issued only in the name of KAISTEEL LIMITED.
  • Each payment must correspond to a unique PI or contract reference, buyer legal name, agreed product configuration, amount and currency.
  • For sample orders, a secure payment link may be offered for the product value and agreed packing charge; the applicable sample-credit policy is set out in the written sample quotation.
  • For container orders, bank transfer is the standard payment route; where a card payment is agreed, a payment link is issued only against an approved PI and agreed payment milestone.
  • KAISTEEL does not request payment to an individual, an unrelated third party or an undisclosed manufacturing supplier.
Sample orders use a default of 50% deposit and 50% balance after pre-shipment inspection, before dispatch. The written PI or contract governs if it differs. A complimentary field-replaceable electrical wear-parts kit is included in each crate. Within warranty, failed core electrical components are replaced free of charge and shipped from China by express air courier within several days; the buyer pays the air freight. On the aluminium motorized lift-up reference configuration, supply voltage is configurable by market (110V/60Hz or 220V/50Hz) and electrical components are rated IP65. For stainless-shell units and other typologies, voltage and ingress protection are confirmed on the approved order specification. See also Warranty, Spares & Sample Terms and the PRO / MAX / CUSTOM Cut Sheet.

4. Technical freeze, changes and cancellation

A deposit does not by itself authorize production. Production begins after the relevant technical scope, drawings, BOM, material choices, appliance interfaces and compliance requirements are approved in writing (Technical Freeze).

Cancellation or change requests must be sent to sales@kaisteelkitchen.com. Before Technical Freeze, KAISTEEL will assess the request and may deduct documented third-party, engineering, sample, material or other work already performed. After Technical Freeze, made-to-order goods may not be cancelled or changed without KAISTEEL's written agreement, because material procurement and production capacity may have been committed. Any approved refund is returned to the original payer or payment method after the relevant order and settlement records have been confirmed.

5. Delivery and risk transfer

The current product-price basis is EXW the named place, Incoterms® 2020. Other terms, including FOB, CIF or DDP, require feasible logistics and KAISTEEL’s written approval for that order. DDP is not offered for Australia. The named port or address and responsibility split must be stated in the approved quotation, PI or contract. Products ship from China; there is no overseas stock or warehouse. Freight, insurance, import clearance, duties, taxes, destination-country compliance, site works and final local electrical, gas and plumbing connections are excluded unless expressly included in the agreed order documents.

Quotation validity and DDP

The written quotation and applicable terms govern validity and delivery obligations. Prices and availability require reconfirmation after expiry; an old quotation is not renewed by a website update.

DDP is quoted for the exact destination, configuration, crate data and delivery conditions. The written scope identifies included import clearance, duties/taxes and final-mile services. Any permitted government-charge adjustment before customs entry requires documented written notice under the quotation. No fixed tariff rate is guaranteed.

Unless expressly included, the customer arranges and pays for forklift unloading, positioning, assembly, installation, permits and qualified utility connections. Remote or difficult access, waiting, redelivery and special handling require separate confirmation.

Returns and exchanges

Confirmed custom-made and project orders are generally not eligible for return or exchange due to change of mind. Manufacturing defects, shortages and shipping-related issues are handled under the applicable sales agreement and KAISTEEL Limited Warranty.

6. Questions, disputes and refunds

For a payment query, cancellation, refund request or commercial dispute, contact sales@kaisteelkitchen.com and quote the PI or contract number. Please retain the PI, payment confirmation and relevant correspondence so that the request can be identified and reviewed promptly.

Sample credit

Sample PIs and equipment credit are available to registered companies worldwide, without model, equipment-value or intended-use restrictions. The sample-order window is 1 September–31 December 2026, based on actual deposit receipt. A brand or group may receive credit only once globally. Credit represents one equipment unit, averaged for multi-unit first orders, and is limited to the payable pre-shipment balance with no cash value, refund or carry-over. Qualifying orders are one or more full 40HQ containers for one customer (one paying entity); multi-customer consolidation is ineligible. A first-order full 40HQ can use credit against its own pre-shipment balance without POD or a 180-day wait. Otherwise, the subsequent qualifying deposit must actually arrive by Day 180 at 23:59 local time at the sample PI final receiving location; Day 1 is the day after carrier-POD delivery there. Equipment-only credit excludes other charges. Takes effect on 11 November 2026, 00:00 HKT (UTC+8). Read the full sample-credit conditions and excluded charges.
