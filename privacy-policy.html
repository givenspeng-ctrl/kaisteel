Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 1 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KAISTEEL LIMITED ("KAISTEEL", "we", "us"), a company incorporated in Hong Kong (BRN/UBI 78595271), collects, uses and protects information submitted through kaisteelkitchen.com (the "Site").

This is a general-purpose policy template prepared for a B2B trade website. It is not a substitute for legal advice — before publishing, we recommend review by counsel familiar with the data-protection requirements of the specific markets we serve (including the EU/UK GDPR and Australia's Privacy Act), since obligations can depend on where enquirers are located and how the Site's hosting and any future analytics tools are configured.

1. What we collect

The Site is a static informational and enquiry site. We do not operate a checkout, account system, or payment collection. Information we may receive includes:

  • RFQ form submissions: when you use the "Request a Configuration Review" form, the details you enter are transmitted securely to our form-processing provider, Formspree, and delivered to KAISTEEL. The fields may include your name, company, business email, website, destination market, buyer type, model interest, estimated quantity, appliance certification market, target delivery date and project notes.
  • Enquiry attribution: the RFQ may also include the landing page, referring page, campaign parameters supplied in the link and the most recent contact or document action in that browser session. We use this only to understand which buyer resource led to the enquiry and to respond in context.
  • Direct correspondence: information you send us by email or WhatsApp, including any CAD drawings, project documents or photographs you choose to attach.
  • Basic technical data: standard web server or hosting-platform logs (e.g. IP address, browser type, pages requested) that our hosting provider may generate as part of normal operation.

2. Cookies and tracking

The Site does not currently run third-party advertising trackers or set advertising cookies. It may use browser session storage to retain first-touch campaign and enquiry-intent information until the browser session ends, so that an RFQ can be attributed to the relevant buyer resource. If we later add cookie-based analytics or advertising tools, we will update this policy and request consent where required.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries, prepare quotations and configuration reviews, and manage the B2B sales process.
  • To administer warranty registration and after-sales support for confirmed orders.
  • To maintain records required for export documentation, compliance dossiers and accounting.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not use enquiry data for unrelated marketing without your consent.

4. Sharing

We do not sell enquiry or project information. We share it only with: (a) our approved manufacturing partner, to the extent needed to prepare a quotation, technical drawing or production order; (b) service providers that operate the Site, hosting infrastructure or RFQ form processing, including Formspree, subject to their applicable terms and safeguards; or (c) authorities or advisers where required by law or necessary to protect KAISTEEL's legal rights.

5. Retention

We retain enquiry and order-related information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above, including applicable commercial, warranty and tax record-keeping periods, after which it is deleted or anonymized where practicable.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information you have sent us, or to object to certain uses. To make a request, contact sales@kaisteelkitchen.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law.

7. International transfers

As a Hong Kong-registered company working with a manufacturing partner in mainland China and buyers worldwide, information you send us may be processed outside your home jurisdiction. We take reasonable steps to keep information secure regardless of where it is processed.

8. Governing law

This Policy is governed by the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, subject to any mandatory data-protection law of the jurisdiction where you are located that cannot lawfully be excluded.
