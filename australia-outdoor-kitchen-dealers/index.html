Australian supply path

Put certification responsibility on the exact components that need it.

The lowest-friction route is usually to combine a controlled KAISTEEL structure with buyer-selected appliances that already carry the approvals required for Australia.

System Shell

Structure, cabinetry, worktop and agreed non-appliance components, prepared for locally sourced and certified appliances.

Appliance-Ready

Approved appliance openings, interfaces and coordination details are frozen against the buyer's nominated models before production.

Full Package

Evaluated only after exact appliance model, voltage or gas type, certificate scope and destination requirements are confirmed in writing.

Certification boundary: KAISTEEL does not itself hold CE, UL, SAA or CSA marks for the complete system. Applicable marks and certificates belong to exact appliances or components and must be matched to the approved model, rating and Australian destination requirements. Final utility connections are completed by appropriately licensed local tradespeople.

Dealer questions

Essential answers before an introductory call.

Can Australian dealers use locally certified appliances?

Yes. Under the System Shell or Appliance-Ready path, openings and utility interfaces can be coordinated around buyer-selected appliances carrying the approvals required for the exact Australian market and installation.

Can I start with one display unit?

Yes. A one-unit display sample is available. The eligible sample product value may be credited as a non-cash invoice deduction against the first qualifying 40'HQ order placed within 180 calendar days, subject to the published policy and quotation.
