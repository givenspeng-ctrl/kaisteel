Choose your model series

PRO

Overall width about 2200–2300 mm. The aluminium lift-up reference is 2300 × 880 × 2400 mm; a ~2250 mm width and a ~730 mm or ~960 mm depth may be specified. Confirm the appliance requirements and supply scope for your project.

MAX

Overall width about 2700–2900 mm. The aluminium lift-up reference is 2900 × 960 × 2400 mm; an 880 mm depth is also available, and a ~2850 mm width and a ~730 mm or ~960 mm depth may be specified. MAX is a series name, not the largest available size.

CUSTOM

All other sizes and typologies, including low-profile units about 1150–1200 mm high, extended typologies about 3500 mm wide, and terrace or open-frame layouts. Names may include the width in millimetres, for example CUSTOM 3500. CUSTOM is quoted per project.

Use the same series name and the approved width in the quotation, pro forma invoice (PI) and drawings. Exterior options are aluminium 2.0 mm or a stainless-steel inner and outer shell. Worktops are SUS304 solid in 6, 8 or 10 mm, or quartz / sintered stone. On the aluminium motorized lift-up reference configuration, supply voltage is configurable by market (110V/60Hz or 220V/50Hz) and electrical components are rated IP65; otherwise voltage and ingress protection are confirmed on the approved order specification. Other dimensions and powered opening and closing arrangements follow the approved configuration. Download the PRO / MAX / CUSTOM Cut Sheet.

Choose your supply scope

Model and supply scope are separate choices. Each scope below must be confirmed for the selected model and project.

System Shell

Main structure, interior and exterior finishes, powered lifting/opening mechanisms, lighting and control systems. Excludes internal cabinetry, worktops, equipment and appliances.

Appliance-Ready

System Shell plus internal cabinetry, doors, worktops, sink, faucet and shelving, with interfaces reserved for specified appliance models. Appliances are not automatically included.

Full Package

Appliance-Ready plus agreed internal appliances and equipment. Only the agreed items are included; exact models, factory integration and destination applicability are confirmed in the approved BOM and PI.

Match the scope to your plan

Start with System Shell if you plan the interior fit-out separately. Choose Appliance-Ready for cabinetry and interfaces while arranging appliances separately. Choose Full Package when you want agreed appliances included in the supply.

Confirm the project details

Share the intended appliance models and site requirements. Openings, interfaces, electricity and voltage, gas type, ventilation, water supply and drainage require review before production. Appliance certificates do not certify the complete kitchen system.

The approved bill of materials (BOM), drawings and pro forma invoice (PI) determine materials, dimensions and final scope. Site assembly, utility connections and local approvals require appropriately qualified local professionals.

Quotation and delivery

Quotations are prepared for the agreed project scope. The default basis is EXW (Incoterms 2020), with the named place in the quotation. Other terms require feasible logistics and KAISTEEL’s written approval for that order. DDP is not offered for Australia.

Products ship from China. Freight, insurance, customs, duties, taxes, importer responsibilities and final-mile delivery are allocated in the written quotation, PI or contract. Unloading, installation, utility connections and site work are not automatically included.

Payment and production

Payment amounts, proportions and milestones are set in the KAISTEEL-approved quotation, PI or contract for each order. The agreed deposit must be received before scheduling. Final configuration and technical requirements need approval before production. Pay the remaining balance in full before shipment.

Product specific warranty

Warranty starts on the shipment date. Coverage follows the product-specific warranty and ordered components, subject to applicable terms and law. Third-party appliances follow their manufacturers’ applicable warranties.
