U.S. 2026 Sample Equipment Credit Program

Evaluate one standard PRO or MAX unit before committing to a qualifying container order.

Reviewed 29 September 2026 · Commercial terms remain subject to the written quotation
Home / Sample credit

The U.S. 2026 Sample Equipment Credit Program applies to qualifying standard PRO/MAX sample orders confirmed September 1–December 31, 2026. Equipment value may be credited in full against a subsequent full-container equipment order, used within 6 months from sample shipment, subject to the formal quotation/PI.

Start with one sample

Qualified new partners may begin with one standard PRO or MAX demo/sample unit for a showroom or local market test. This is not a universal MOQ of one. CUSTOM quantity and sample feasibility require engineering review.

Order and credit dates

Qualifying sample orders must be confirmed September 1–December 31, 2026. The equipment credit must be used within 6 months from the sample unit shipment date.

Eligible value

The equipment value of one qualifying standard PRO or MAX sample may be credited in full against the equipment value of a subsequent full-container order. This is equipment credit, not a cash refund or a free sample.

Excluded costs

Packaging, freight, duties/taxes, custom engineering and installation are excluded. The formal quotation or PI confirms eligibility and commercial terms.

Other markets and order support

This published schedule summarizes the U.S. dealer brief. Sample-credit availability for other markets and non-standard configurations must be confirmed in writing. There is no fixed MOQ across all orders; configuration, customization and the distribution or project plan determine quantity.

For defects, shortages or transit issues, retain photographs, packaging and order records and contact sales@kaisteelkitchen.com. The sales agreement and applicable limited warranty govern handling.
