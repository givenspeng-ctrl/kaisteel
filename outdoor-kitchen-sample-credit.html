How the sample product-value credit works

Evaluate one standard PRO or MAX unit before committing to a qualifying container order.

Reviewed 2 September 2026 · Commercial terms remain subject to the written quotation
Home / Resources / Sample credit

Qualified B2B buyers may purchase one sample unit. The eligible sample product value can be deducted from the first qualifying container invoice when every condition below is satisfied.

Credit window

The qualifying order must be placed within 180 calendar days after documented delivery of the sample.

Qualifying order

The default qualifying order is one 40'HQ placed by the same verified legal entity. A 20'GP may qualify only when the written quotation expressly approves the exception and available credit.

What is credited

Up to 100% of the eligible sample product value shown on the original invoice, applied as a non-cash invoice deduction.

What is excluded

Freight, insurance, duties, taxes, clearance, inland delivery, installation, custom engineering and the USD 150 LCL wooden-crate charge.

Transit-damage procedure

  1. Inspect the package and unit promptly and preserve the original packaging.
  2. Record visible damage on the carrier delivery record where practicable.
  3. Email photographs and supporting records within seven calendar days after delivery.
  4. For an approved claim, eligible replacement parts carry no product charge.
  5. Target replacement-part production is 7-10 working days after written approval; international transit time is excluded.
Freight, insurance, duties, taxes, clearance and removal or reinstallation remain the buyer's responsibility unless otherwise agreed in writing.
