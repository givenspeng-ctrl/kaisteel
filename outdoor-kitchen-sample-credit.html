Global 2026 Sample Equipment Credit Program

Registered-company sample equipment credit, with separate first-container and subsequent-container routes.

This policy update takes effect on 11 November 2026, 00:00 HKT (UTC+8). Existing orders remain governed by their agreed documents and applicable statutory rights.
Home / Sample credit

Eligibility and participation

Sample PIs and credit are issued only to registered business entities (companies) worldwide, without model, equipment-value or intended-use restrictions. A brand or group may receive the credit only once globally, with no repeat claim in another country or region or through another brand. Identify the first order by the applicant’s first contract and first payment. The sample-order window is 1 September through 31 December 2026. Both sample and subsequent qualifying order dates mean actual deposit receipt, not a payment instruction or remittance advice; these dates do not define legal contract formation.

One equipment unit, not the whole order

For a single-unit first order, use that unit’s equipment purchase price. For a multi-unit first order, divide total first-order equipment value by equipment-unit count. For a full-40HQ first order, divide that first container order’s equipment value by equipment-unit count. Credit is capped at the applicable payable pre-shipment balance: no negative balance, refund, cash value or carry-over. Equipment value and total order price must be itemized separately; do not infer an equipment/packing split from a package price.

Two credit routes

Qualifying containers are one or more full 40HQ containers for one customer (one paying entity). Multi-customer consolidation is ineligible. If the first order itself is a full 40HQ, credit may be applied to that same order’s pre-shipment balance without waiting for delivery, POD or the 180-day period.

Subsequent-container deadline

For a first order that is not a full 40HQ, actual delivery must be to the final receiving address in the sample PI, evidenced by the carrier’s POD. Arrival at the destination port alone does not count. The next day is Day 1. The subsequent qualifying full-container order deposit must actually be received by 23:59 local time at that PI-specified final receiving location on Day 180. No anti-delay, mandatory-collection, deemed-arrival or deemed-receipt clause applies.

Excluded charges

Sample or LCL surcharges; packaging and packing upgrades; freight, insurance, customs clearance, duties and taxes; custom engineering, upgrades, installation and separately charged services are excluded from equipment credit.

Payment and warranty

Payment amounts, proportions and milestones are determined per order in the KAISTEEL-approved quotation, PI or contract. The agreed deposit must actually be received before production scheduling. Eligible credit is deducted from the pre-shipment balance, and the remaining balance must be paid in full before shipment. Warranty starts on shipment and is administered separately from sample credit; the credit policy does not expand warranty coverage.

Written participation record

The formal quotation or PI must record participation, the equipment value and the credit calculation. Sample credit is confirmed in writing for the order; it is not granted automatically by submitting an enquiry.

Commencement

This policy update takes effect on 11 November 2026, 00:00 HKT (UTC+8). Existing orders remain governed by their agreed documents and applicable statutory rights. Materials, dimensions, quantities, packing and lead times follow the approved BOM, drawings and PI.
