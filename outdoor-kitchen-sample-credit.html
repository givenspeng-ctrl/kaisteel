Global sample equipment credit

Available worldwide for qualifying standard PRO or MAX sample orders placed on or before December 31, 2026, subject to express approval in the quotation/PI. Only applicable equipment value explicitly itemized in the quotation/PI may be credited against the equipment value of a qualifying subsequent full-container order within 6 months after sample shipment. This is non-cash purchase credit, not a refund or free sample. Sample/LCL surcharges, packaging, freight, insurance, clearance, duties/taxes, custom engineering, upgrades and installation are excluded. Qualified new partners may start with one standard sample; CUSTOM requires separate engineering review and written approval.

Materials, dimensions, quantities, packing and lead times follow the approved BOM, drawings and PI. This policy update does not retrospectively amend signed orders or earlier contractual commitments.

No automatic sample surcharge is imposed by this policy. The written quotation/PI must state participation, eligible equipment amount and deadline. For defects, shortages or transit issues, retain photographs, packaging and order records; the sales agreement and applicable warranty govern handling.
